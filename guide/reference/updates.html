Updates and uninstalling ​

Leathercase does not update itself behind your back. It asks whether a newer version exists only when you say so, downloads it only when you take it, and installs it when you restart. This page covers how that works on each system, where to read what changed, and how to uninstall the app, including what stays behind.

Checking for a newer version ​

Nothing goes online until you ask. A fresh install has never checked, and Check when Leathercase opens is off.

You can check in four ways:

  • Settings, Updates, Check now. Open Settings with Ctrl+, (Cmd+, on macOS) or from Settings at the foot of the sidebar.
  • Settings, About, Check now. About Leathercase shows this copy's version with the same row as Updates; open it from the Leathercase menu on macOS, from Help on Windows and Linux, or with About Leathercase in the find field.
  • The File menu, Check for updates.
  • The find field: press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K), type "Check for updates" and press Enter.

Or let Leathercase check each time it opens: turn on Check when Leathercase opens in Settings, Updates. The hint says what that does: Off until you turn it on. When a newer version is out, a dot on Settings says so; nothing downloads by itself. The choice is this computer's, kept in its preferences.

When a check finds a newer version, a dot appears on Settings in the sidebar and on Updates in the Settings list, both saying Version 0.25.0 is out (with the real number).

A downloaded update waits. Keep working; it installs when you choose Restart now, or by itself the next time you quit Leathercase. Either way Leathercase first finishes what it was writing (the sentence log, the mate's state, your preferences), waiting at most two seconds, and then quits.

The updater's downloads are kept in a cache folder of their own (see Uninstalling) and do not touch your writing home.

Installing a new version by hand ​

Every release is on the releases page. Download the file for your system and install it over the old version; your homes, preferences and stories stay as they were, since none of them live inside the app.

Windows (x64 and Arm)leathercase-<version>-win.exe

macOS, Intelleathercase-<version>-mac-x64.dmg

Each release also lists a SHA256SUMS file per system, so you can check that the file you downloaded is the one published.

Windows asks before the installer runs

The Windows installer is not yet signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway. The macOS app is signed and notarised and opens without a warning on macOS 12 or later.

What changed in a version ​

Each release on the releases page carries its notes: what changed in that version, followed by a table of which file to download. The app does not show release notes itself; Every version and its notes, on Settings, About, opens the releases page in your browser, as does Open download page.

Uninstalling ​

Uninstalling removes the app. It never touches your writing: your notes stay where they are, and so do the files Leathercase kept beside them, so you can read them without the app or open them again after a reinstall. The same guide is in the app: Settings, Updates, How to uninstall.

Before you uninstall: Leathercase's files in your homes ​

Leathercase keeps its own files in each writing home: Suite.md and suite/ at its root, Writer/ for your cards, and a suite/ folder inside each story's folder (see Files Leathercase writes). Uninstalling leaves them, on purpose: they are yours, and they hold your grid, threads, cards and comments.

To remove them too, do it while the app is still installed: in Settings, Storage, choose Remove… beside each home, then Purge. Purge moves Leathercase's files to the trash and leaves your notes alone. Or delete those folders yourself at any time.

If you connected an agent, remove the Leathercase bridge from that agent's app as well: in Claude Desktop, under Settings, Extensions; for the others, the line you added to their settings (see Connect an agent).

Windows ​

  1. Quit Leathercase.
  2. Open Settings, Apps, Installed apps. Find Leathercase, open its menu, and choose Uninstall. On older Windows, open Control Panel, Programs and Features.
  3. The uninstaller asks whether to remove this computer's settings as well. Answer Yes to remove them; answer No to keep them for a later reinstall.

This computer's settings are the list of your homes, your preferences, connected agents and the downloaded search model. They live in %APPDATA%\Leathercase. The uninstaller also removes the leathercase:// link and the updater's downloads in %LOCALAPPDATA%\@leathercasedesktop-updater.

When Leathercase was installed for all users and Windows asked another account, an administrator, to approve the uninstall, the uninstaller works in that administrator's account. Its question then removes that account's settings, not yours, and your link stays registered. To remove your settings, sign in to your own account and delete %APPDATA%\Leathercase yourself.

To uninstall without questions, as an administrator would, run the uninstaller with /S; add --delete-app-data to remove this computer's settings too. This is where it is when Leathercase was installed for you alone in the default place; otherwise it is in the folder you chose:

"%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\leathercase\Uninstall leathercase.exe" /S --delete-app-data

A Leathercase updated from a version before 0.30 stays in the folder it was first installed in, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\creative-suite, and its uninstaller is there.

macOS ​

macOS apps have no uninstaller.

  1. Quit Leathercase.
  2. In Finder, open Applications and drag Leathercase to the Trash. Empty the Trash.

The app's settings stay behind in your Library folder. To remove them too, in Finder choose Go, Go to Folder, and delete each of these that exists:

~/Library/Application Support/Leathercase
~/Library/Caches/@leathercasedesktop-updater
~/Library/Caches/app.leathercase.desktop
~/Library/Caches/app.leathercase.desktop.ShipIt
~/Library/HTTPStorages/app.leathercase.desktop
~/Library/Preferences/app.leathercase.desktop.plist
~/Library/Saved Application State/app.leathercase.desktop.savedState

Or in Terminal:

rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/Leathercase ~/Library/Caches/@leathercasedesktop-updater ~/Library/Caches/app.leathercase.desktop* ~/Library/HTTPStorages/app.leathercase.desktop ~/Library/Preferences/app.leathercase.desktop.plist ~/Library/Saved\ Application\ State/app.leathercase.desktop.savedState

Linux ​

How you remove the app depends on how you installed it.

If you installed the .deb package (Debian, Ubuntu, Mint, Pop!_OS), remove it from your software centre, or from a terminal:

sudo apt remove leathercase

If you run the AppImage, it was never installed: delete the .AppImage file. If a tool such as Gear Lever or AppImageLauncher added it to your menu, remove it there as well.

The AppImage adds two files the first time it runs, so leathercase:// links reach it: a menu entry and its icon. Delete them with the AppImage:

rm -f ~/.local/share/applications/leathercase.desktop ~/.local/share/icons/hicolor/512x512/apps/leathercase.png

Either way, the app's settings stay behind. To remove them too:

rm -rf ~/.config/Leathercase ~/.cache/@leathercasedesktop-updater

What versions before 0.30 left behind ​

Before 0.30 the app had another name, and it kept its settings in other folders. Leathercase moves the settings to its own folder the first time it opens, so what is left in the old places can go. On Windows and Linux, 0.30 arrived as an ordinary update; on macOS it had to be downloaded once by hand, because the app's identity changed with its name, so the old app may still be in Applications.

On Windows, delete %APPDATA%\@creative-suite and %LOCALAPPDATA%\@creative-suitedesktop-updater if they are still there.

On macOS, in Terminal:

rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/@creative-suite ~/Library/Caches/@creative-suitedesktop-updater ~/Library/Caches/com.creativesuite.desktop* ~/Library/HTTPStorages/com.creativesuite.desktop ~/Library/Preferences/com.creativesuite.desktop.plist ~/Library/Saved\ Application\ State/com.creativesuite.desktop.savedState

On Linux, the .deb of 0.30 replaces the old package. The old AppImage's menu entry and its settings can go:

rm -f ~/.local/share/applications/creative-suite.desktop ~/.local/share/icons/hicolor/512x512/apps/creative-suite.png
rm -rf ~/.config/@creative-suite ~/.cache/@creative-suitedesktop-updater
